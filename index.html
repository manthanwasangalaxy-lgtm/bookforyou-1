City guides

Flights to Australia's Major Cities

Australia is a large country, and choosing the right arrival airport is an important part of planning your journey. International travellers commonly fly into Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane or Perth, depending on where they plan to spend their time.

Flights to Sydney

Sydney is one of Australia's best-known international destinations. It attracts travellers looking for beaches, famous landmarks, city experiences and opportunities to explore New South Wales.

When planning flights to Sydney, travellers can compare different departure dates, airlines and connecting routes. Being flexible with your travel schedule may provide more options when selecting a suitable flight to Sydney.

Sydney is also a popular starting point for longer Australian holidays, particularly for travellers planning to visit multiple cities.

Flights to Melbourne

Melbourne is known for its culture, food, sporting events and diverse city lifestyle. It is a popular destination for holidays, business travel and visiting family.

Travellers planning a journey to Melbourne should consider the overall route, available connections and travel duration. Depending on the departure airport, some journeys may involve a stopover.

Comparing different airlines and schedules can help you find an option that fits your travel plans for flights to Melbourne.

Flights to Brisbane

Brisbane is a popular destination for travellers visiting Queensland. The city provides access to beaches, attractions and other destinations across the region.

When searching for flights to Brisbane, it can be useful to compare different travel dates and routes. A small amount of flexibility may open up additional flight options.

Flights to Perth

Perth is located on Australia's west coast and is a popular destination for holidays, work and family travel. Its location can mean that the most suitable route depends heavily on where you are departing from.

Travellers can compare connecting flights, travel times and airline options before choosing their flight to Perth.

Trip planning

Planning Your Australia Trip

A successful journey involves more than simply choosing a flight to Australia. Before booking, think about where you want to go and how long you plan to stay.

Australia is a large destination, and travelling between cities can take significant time. Your arrival airport should ideally match your itinerary.

For example, travellers focusing on New South Wales may prefer Sydney, while those visiting Victoria may find Melbourne more convenient. Queensland travellers may choose Brisbane, while Perth can be suitable for plans focused on Western Australia.

If you plan to visit multiple destinations, consider the cost and convenience of travelling between cities after your arrival.

Comparison tips

Tips for Comparing Flight Options

Comparing flights to Australia can help you understand the different options available for your journey.

Here are some useful factors to consider:

  • ✓Compare Travel Dates: If your schedule allows flexibility, checking different dates can help you see a wider selection of available options.
  • ✓Consider Alternative Airports: Depending on where you live and where you are travelling in Australia, alternative departure or arrival airports may provide different routes.
  • ✓Check the Total Journey Time: The cheapest-looking option is not always the most convenient. A long stopover can significantly increase the total travel time.
  • ✓Review Baggage Allowances: Different airlines and ticket types may include different baggage allowances. Always check what is included before confirming your booking.
  • ✓Look at Ticket Conditions: Flexible tickets, changes and cancellations can have different conditions depending on the fare type.
